# AI × Live Music Performance, Human–AI Co-Creativity, Music-Driven Visuals, and the Economics of AI-Augmented Creativity (2016–2026) — Citation Notes for "Embodied AI Creativity: Measuring the Economic Impact of XR Drummer Performances"

Scope note: compiled 2026-10-14 via web search. Every entry carries a URL. DOIs marked **[verified]** were confirmed by at least one search-returned source (publisher, repository, OpenAlex, dblp, Zenodo/NIME listing). DOIs marked **[unverified]** are from the researcher's recall and could not be checked because doi.org / api.crossref.org were unreachable from the research sandbox — check before citing. ## Key Question 1 — Foundational and recent papers on human–AI co-creativity in music (Magenta/Google, MIT Media Lab/Rudess, Sony CSL/Pachet/Deruty, Sturm, Herndon's Spawn, mixed-initiative creative interfaces)

### Takeaway
There is a clear, citable lineage from steerable generative models (DeepBach 2017; GrooVAE 2019) through HCI "steering tools" for novices (Louie et al. CHI 2020) and practitioner-centred evaluations (Sturm et al. 2019; AI Song Contest 2020; Deruty et al. 2022) to real-time stage systems with a named virtuoso (Rudess's jam_bot, ISMIR 2025). A drummer who performs live with LLM tooling can position himself as the "practitioner-in-the-loop" that this literature repeatedly says is missing.

### Cited Findings
- **Deterding, Hook, Fiebrink, Gillies, Gow, Akten, Smith, Nelson, Lewis / Deterding et al. (2017). "Mixed-Initiative Creative Interfaces." CHI EA '17, pp. 628–635. DOI 10.1145/3027063.3027072 [verified via White Rose + CPS-VO].** Workshop paper that coined/popularised "mixed-initiative creative interfaces" (MICI) as a research agenda spanning HCI and games. OA: author manuscript at White Rose. *Why it matters:* gives the proposal its umbrella term for a drummer + Claude/LLM system; author list beyond Deterding not confirmed in sources — verify on ACM DL. — [White Rose record](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/112515); [PDF](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/112515/1/wks0132_deterdingA.pdf); [CPS-VO](https://archive.cps-vo.org/node/55349)
- **Hadjeres, Pachet & Nielsen (2017). "DeepBach: a Steerable Model for Bach Chorales Generation." ICML 2017, PMLR 70:1362–1371. arXiv:1612.01010.** Graphical model with pseudo-Gibbs sampling; users steer output by fixing notes/rhythms/cadences. OA (PMLR). *Why it matters:* canonical Pachet-era "steerable" generative model; the Flow Machines/Sony CSL affiliation was NOT confirmed in search results (check PDF affiliations). — [PMLR](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/hadjeres17a.html); [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.01010)
- **Gillick, Roberts, Engel, Eck & Bamman (2019). "Learning to Groove with Inverse Sequence Transformations." ICML 2019, PMLR 97:2269–2279. arXiv:1905.06118.** Introduces GrooVAE and the Groove MIDI Dataset: 13.6 h of aligned MIDI/audio human drumming recorded on a Roland TD-11 V-Drum kit; tasks Humanization, Infilling, Tap2Drum. OA. *Why it matters:* the single most important Magenta paper for a drummer — the dataset itself is e-drum performance data, directly comparable to drum-hit streams in an XR rig. — [PMLR](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/gillick19a.html); [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.06118v2); [Groove dataset](https://magenta.tensorflow.org/datasets/groove)
- **Mann, Gillick, Dinculescu, Radebaugh & Hawthorne (2019). "Magenta Studio: Augmenting Creativity with Deep Learning in Ableton Live." Proc. Int. Workshop on Musical Metacreation (MUME) 2019.** Five Ableton plugins (Generate, Continue, Interpolate, Groove, Drumify) built on Electron/TensorFlow.js/Max for Live. OA (Google Research page). *Why it matters:* shows AI drum tooling delivered inside producer workflows; note it is a MUME paper, not NIME, and Roberts is not a listed author despite frequent "Roberts et al. 2019" citations. — [Google Research](https://research.google/pubs/pub48280); [Magenta announcement](https://magenta.tensorflow.org/studio-announce)
- **Louie, Coenen, Huang, Terry & Cai (2020). "Novice-AI Music Co-Creation via AI-Steering Tools for Deep Generative Models." CHI 2020. DOI 10.1145/3313831.3376739 [verified].** Steering tools let novices build songs section-by-section, giving more control than whole-piece generation. *Why it matters:* standard CHI citation for "steerability" as a co-creativity design value. — [Magenta citation page](https://magenta.tensorflow.org/people-first-hci-ml-collaborations)
- **Huang, Koops, Newton-Rex, Dinculescu & Cai (2020). "AI Song Contest: Human-AI Co-Creation in Songwriting." ISMIR 2020. arXiv:2010.05388.** Survey of 13 teams / 61 participants; teams used modular pipelines of small models and mass-generate-then-curate strategies because models were not steerable; argues interfaces should be decomposable, steerable, interpretable, adaptive. OA. *Why it matters:* empirical account of how real musicians actually work with AI; author list differs across sources (Google page omits Huang/Cai) — cite from ISMIR proceedings. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.05388); [ISMIR poster](https://program.ismir2020.net/static/posters/167.pdf)
- **Sturm, Ben-Tal, Monaghan, Collins, Herremans, Chew, Hadjeres, Deruty & Pachet (2019). "Machine Learning Research that Matters for Music Creation: A Case Study." Journal of New Music Research 48(1):36–55. DOI 10.1080/09298215.2018.1515233 [verified].** Argues most music-ML is judged by likelihoods/listening tests, not practitioner usefulness; authors built applications with practitioners and staged a public concert, then reflected. OA author manuscript (Kingston). *Why it matters:* legitimises "a public concert is a research output" — key framing for performance-as-research. — [Kingston eprints](https://eprints.kingston.ac.uk/id/eprint/41834); [AAM PDF](https://eprints.kingston.ac.uk/41834/6/Ben-Tal-O-41834-AAM.pdf)

OA (TISMIR is fully OA). *Why it matters:* Sony CSL is a Tokyo-adjacent institution (Sony CSL has a Tokyo lab) — useful for Japanese grant framing. — [TISMIR](https://transactions.ismir.net/articles/10.5334/tismir.100); [dblp](https://dblp.org/pid/93/4381)

DOI 10.5281/zenodo.17811478 [verified via MIT Media Lab listing].** Real-time MIDI-in/MIDI-out music-LM system built to match Jordan Rudess's improvisational style; premiered Sept 2024 at MIT Media Lab ("Jordan and the jam_bot", 6 pieces); Rudess became Distinguished Visiting Artist Sept 2025; concerts at MIT Oct 3 2026. OA (Zenodo). *Why it matters:* the closest peer-reviewed precedent for "virtuoso instrumentalist + AI band member on stage" with an elite venue; ideal comparator for an XR drummer. — [MIT Media Lab pub](https://www.media.mit.edu/publications/the-jam_bot-a-real-time-system-for-collaborative-free-improvisation-with-music-language-models/); [ISMIR 2025 program](https://ismir2025program.ismir.net/music_5.html); [MIT Arts](https://arts.mit.edu/jordan-and-the-jam_bot/); [MIDI.org award](https://midi.org/innovation-award/the-jam_bot)
- **Roberts & Krueger (Exeter, forthcoming in *Collaborative Embodied Performance*). "Musical agency and collaboration in the digital age."** Philosophical chapter testing Herndon's claim that Spawn (neural net on *PROTO*, 2019) is a legitimate fourth collaborator. OA preprint in Exeter ORE. *Why it matters:* the main academic treatment of Holly Herndon/Spawn; cite as "forthcoming/unverified publication details". — [Exeter ORE](https://ore.exeter.ac.uk/repository/handle/10871/127636?show=full); [PDF](https://ore.exeter.ac.uk/rest/bitstreams/182232/retrieve)

DOI 10.1145/3555578 [verified].** Interviews with 5 internationally renowned artists: AI treated as *material* whose unpredictability is used deliberately; artists resist AI-research culture. OA via HAL. *Why it matters:* strongest HCI citation for "artist as independent researcher who treats AI as material" — note it is CSCW, not CHI. — [HAL](https://hal-lara.archives-ouvertes.fr/HCI-SORBONNE/hal-03762351v1)

OA (nime.org). *Why it matters:* shows NIME accepts prompt-driven live AI improvisation case studies — close to a Claude-in-the-loop drum performance. — [NIME 2025](https://nime.org/proc/nime2025_54/)
- **ReaLJam (2025). "ReaLJam: Real-Time Human-AI Music Jamming with Reinforcement Learning-Tuned Transformers." arXiv:2502.21267.** User study with 6 experienced musicians performing live, plus interviews. OA. ### Inferences
- A grant narrative can chain: steerable models (2017–19) → steering interfaces (2020) → practitioner evaluation (2019–22) → stage deployment with named virtuoso (2025) → *gap*: no peer-reviewed study of a drummer/percussionist using an LLM agent plus XR visuals with audience/economic outcome measures.
- The MIT jam_bot and Herndon/Spawn cases show that institutional legitimacy came from pairing a recognised artist with a lab; an independent researcher can emulate this by co-authoring with a university lab (e.g., Sony CSL Tokyo, Keio/Tokyo Tech media labs) — inference, not a sourced fact.

### Gaps
- No search surfaced a peer-reviewed paper on LLM (text-model) agents, as opposed to music-LMs, acting as live band members; this appears to be a genuine gap as of Oct 2026.
- Flow Machines / "Daddy's Car" (Pachet, Sony CSL 2016) was not located with a stable URL in this session; cite Pachet, Roy & Carré 2020 retrospective only after locating it (unverified).
- Deruty et al. 2022 abstract content was not retrievable; summary above is from bibliographic context only.

## Key Question 2 — Real-time audio-reactive / music-driven generative visuals and VJing research (SIGGRAPH, NIME, ISEA)

### Takeaway
Peer-reviewed work on *live* AI-driven music visualisation is thin and mostly outside SIGGRAPH/NIME proper (xCoAx, ISEA artist talks, Generative Art conference, NeurIPS workshops); the Autolume line (Kraasch & Pasquier, SFU Metacreation Lab) and StreamDiffusion are the two anchors, and both map directly onto a TouchDesigner + StreamDiffusion drum-reactive pipeline.

### Cited Findings
- **Kraasch & Pasquier (2022). "Autolume-Live: Turning GANs into a Live VJing tool." xCoAx 2022, Coimbra.** GAN-based live VJ tool mapping amplitude, pitch, onset strength to latent-space trajectories, controllable by MIDI; paper states deep-learning live audio-driven visuals had not previously been researched. OA PDF. — [xCoAx PDF](https://2022.xcoax.org/pdf/xcoax2022-kraasch.pdf)
- **Kraasch & Pasquier (2022). "Autolume: Automating Live Music Visualization." ISEA2022 artist talk (Barcelona, 14 June 2022).** Same system presented at ISEA; installations at Distopya (Istanbul 2021) and Light-Up Kelowna (2022). *Why it matters:* precedent for ISEA accepting an AI-VJ system as an artist talk — a realistic first ISEA target. — [ISEA archives](https://isea-archives.siggraph.org/?p=71647); [ISEA2022 page](https://isea2022.isea-international.org/?p=3427); [Metacreation Lab](https://www.metacreation.net/projects/autolume-automating-live-music-visualisation)
- **Kraasch (2023). "Autolume-Live: An Interface for Live Visual Performances using GANs." M.Sc. thesis, Simon Fraser University (supervisor P. Pasquier).** OA. No NIME 2023 Autolume paper was found; the NIME attribution is **unverified/likely wrong**. — [SFU thesis](https://summit.sfu.ca/_flysystem/fedora/2023-07/etd22382.pdf); [record](https://summit.sfu.ca/item/36414)
- **Kodaira, Xu et al. (2023). "StreamDiffusion: A Pipeline-level Solution for Real-time Interactive Generation." arXiv:2312.12491.** Stream Batch denoising, residual CFG, stochastic similarity filter; up to 91.07 fps img2img on one RTX 4090; ~59× throughput vs Diffusers AutoPipeline (authors' own benchmarks). OA; code at cumulo-autumn/StreamDiffusion (also a dotsimulate TouchDesigner fork). *Why it matters:* this is the exact engine in the user's TD pipeline; citing it grounds the technical novelty claim of drum-hit-driven real-time diffusion visuals. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.12491v2); [abs](https://web3.arxiv.org/abs/2312.12491?context=cs); [TD fork](https://github.com/dotsimulate/StreamDiffusion)
- **Jeong, Doh & Kwon (2021). "TräumerAI: Dreaming Music with StyleGAN." NeurIPS 2020 Workshop on ML for Creativity and Design; arXiv:2102.04680.** Maps music auto-tagging embeddings to StyleGAN2 (WikiArt) style vectors via human-annotated pairings (100 clips × 200 images); critiques earlier audio-reactive StyleGAN for responding only to loudness/timbre. OA; code jdasam/traeumerAI. *Why it matters:* a Korean/Asia-based precedent for mood-level (not just amplitude-level) music→visual mapping. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.04680v1)
- **Erdem, Wallace & Jensenius (2022). "CAVI: A Coadaptive Audiovisual Instrument–Composition." NIME 2022.** Deep-learning instrument driven by performer EMG/motion data controlling live audio-visual processing; final performance featured a guitarist and a **drummer**. OA (nime.org). — [NIME 2022](https://nime.org/proc/nime2022_25/)
- **Rochais (2022). "A7" — TouchDesigner-based system for real-time visual accompaniment of live electronic music. Generative Art Conference 2022.** OA PDF. *Why it matters:* peer-reviewed precedent for TouchDesigner as a research platform. — [GA2022 PDF](https://generativeart.com/papersGA2022/2022/Guillaume%20Rochais%20ebook2022-49.pdf)
- **Beane (Texas A&M). "Generating audio-responsive video images in real-time for a live symphony performance."** Documents 2006 live audio-reactive visuals with an orchestra; thesis-level, OA in TDL. — [TDL](https://tdl-ir.tdl.org/items/911c91ce-d68e-4780-97d3-9b1760d99a69)
- Universidad Pontificia Comillas final-degree project: CNN/GRU models detect musical events from short audio segments and trigger visuals in TouchDesigner (student work; not peer-reviewed). — [Comillas repo](https://repositorio.comillas.edu/jspui/handle/11531/95114)

### Inferences
- Because the peer-reviewed base is small and recent, a NIME/ISEA paper describing drum-trigger → OSC → TouchDesigner → StreamDiffusion with latency measurements and audience evaluation would be publishable as a *system + evaluation* contribution rather than needing a new model.

### Gaps
- No SIGGRAPH (Art Papers or technical) paper on real-time AI music visualisation was surfaced in this session; search ACM DL "SIGGRAPH Art Papers" 2022–2025 directly.
- No NIME paper specifically on drum-hit-driven generative visuals was found.

## Key Question 3 — Live coding and performer–machine interaction: how researchers evaluate a live AI-augmented performance

### Takeaway
Evaluation in this field is mixed-method and small-N: performer interviews + audience questionnaires around public performances (Erdem et al. 2022; Hsu & Sosnick 2009), physiological/confidence signalling studies (McCormack et al. 2019), and creativity-evaluation frameworks (Jordanous 2012 SPECS). A drummer can adopt these directly and add economic outcome measures, which none of them include.

### Cited Findings
- **Erdem, Wallace, Glette & Jensenius (2022). "Tool or Actor? Expert Improvisers' Evaluation of a Musical AI 'Toddler'." Computer Music Journal 46(4):26–42. DOI 10.1162/comj_a_00657 [verified via OpenAlex; OA].** Evaluation of CAVI across two public performances using performer interviews, audience questionnaires and creator self-analysis; perception as tool vs actor correlates with performer's sense of agency. *Why it matters:* the template evaluation protocol for a live AI-augmented drum performance. — [OpenAlex](https://api.openalex.org/works/doi:10.1162%2FCOMJ_A_00657); [UiO DUO](https://www.duo.uio.no/handle/10852/110981); [NIME music program](https://nime.org/proc_music/nime2022_music_22/)
- **Hsu & Sosnick (2009). "Evaluating Interactive Music Systems: An HCI Approach." NIME 2009.** Proposes test designs covering rehearsal and performance environments, capturing both musician-participant and audience-observer experience. OA. — [NIME](https://nime.org/proc/nime2009_hsu/)
- **McCormack, Gifford, Hutchings, Llano Rodriguez, Yee-King & d'Inverno (2019). "In a Silent Way: Communication Between AI and Improvising Musicians Beyond Sound." CHI 2019. arXiv:1902.06442. ACM DOI 10.1145/3290605.3300268 [unverified — from recall].** Collaborative improvising **AI drummer** that shows its confidence via emoticon visualisation; trained on performance data plus real-time skin conductance of musicians improvising with professional drummers; audience perception assessed. OA (arXiv, Monash). *Why it matters:* the one CHI paper where the AI *is a drummer* and extra-musical visual signalling to the audience is the research object — directly analogous to XR visuals as communication channel. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.06442v1); [Monash](https://research.monash.edu/en/publications/in-a-silent-way-communication-between-ai-and-improvising-musician/)
- **Jordanous (2012). "A Standardised Procedure for Evaluating Creative Systems: Computational Creativity Evaluation Based on What it is to be Creative." Cognitive Computation 4(3):246–279. DOI 10.1007/s12559-012-9156-1 [verified via Kent KAR].** SPECS three-step method (define creativity for the system → derive standards → test), demonstrated on music-improvisation systems. *Why it matters:* gives a grant a recognised evaluation framework name. — [Kent KAR](https://kar.kent.ac.uk/42379/)
- **Karimi, Grace, Maher & Davis (2018). "Evaluating Creativity in Computational Co-Creative Systems." ICCC 2018; arXiv:1807.09886.** Survey of evaluation approaches for co-creative (human+AI) systems. OA. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.09886)
- **Burland & McLean (2016) [authors unverified]. Audience study of live coding events. International Journal of Performance Arts and Digital Media. DOI 10.1080/14794713.2016.1227596 [verified via White Rose metadata].** Online questionnaire on audience motivations, experiences and reactions to projected source code. *Why it matters:* model for surveying audiences about *seeing the machine's process* on screen — transferable to showing AI/visual pipelines in XR. — [White Rose](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/109223/)
- **Diapoulis (2023) [author unverified]. "Musical live coding in relation to interactivity variations." Organised Sound. DOI 10.1017/S1355771823000444 [verified].** Observational study of 11 live-coding performance systems. — [Cambridge PDF](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/E0134B8B71E925C7014F59EA411E2406/S1355771823000444a.pdf/musical-live-coding-in-relation-to-interactivity-variations.pdf)
- **Diapoulis & Dahlstedt (2021). "The creative act of live coding practice in music performance." Chalmers.** Conceptual framework for evaluating live-coding systems. OA. — [Chalmers](https://research.chalmers.se/en/publication/526821)
- **"Rehearsal-as-research" methodology (UiO).** Proposes structuring evaluation sessions of free improvisation with NIME performers around improvisation-training practices. OA. — [UiO DUO](https://duo.uio.no/handle/10852/68795)
- **Hopkins et al. (2025). "BrAIn Jam." Frontiers in Computer Science. DOI 10.3389/fcomp.2025.1570249 [from search; verify].** Uses fNIRS to monitor **drummers'** brain states so an AI rhythm partner can adapt. OA. — [Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/computer-science/articles/10.3389/fcomp.2025.1570249/abstract)
- **Lederman, Holland & Mulholland (2020, 2021). PPIG doctoral-consortium papers on "shedding" — mixed-initiative interaction between a human drummer and a conversational agent,** motivated by drummers' limited opportunity to play live with other drummers. OA. — [PPIG 2020](https://ppig.org/papers/2020-ppig-31st-dc-lederman/); [PPIG 2021](https://ppig.org/papers/2021-ppig-32nd-lederman/)
- **Jordà, Gómez-Marín, Faraldo & Herrera (2016). "Drumming with style: From user needs to a working prototype." NIME 2016.** Stylistic drum-pattern generator designed from a producer survey (offline, not live). OA. — [NIME](https://nime.org/proc/nime2016_jordnicode225/)

### Inferences
- The standard protocol a reviewer will expect: ≥2 public performances, semi-structured performer interviews, audience questionnaire (presence/agency/perceived creativity), plus system logs; adding ticket/merch/streaming revenue as dependent variables would be novel.

### Gaps
- No paper was found that evaluates an AI-augmented *XR/mixed-reality* instrumental performance with both HCI and economic measures.

## Key Question 4 — Economics of creativity and AI: measured economic/audience impact of AI-augmented creative work; AI art valuation; cultural economics of live music

### Takeaway
There is now a robust experimental economics literature showing generative AI raises individual creative productivity/quality (Noy & Zhang 2023; Doshi & Hauser 2024; Zhou & Lee 2024; Brynjolfsson et al. 2025) while reducing collective diversity, and a parallel psychology literature showing audiences *discount* work labelled as AI-made (Horton et al. 2023; Shank et al. 2023). Nothing measures economic impact of live, embodied, AI-augmented performance — the exact gap in the proposed study title.

### Cited Findings
- **Noy & Zhang (2023). "Experimental Evidence on the Productivity Effects of Generative Artificial Intelligence." Science 381(6654). DOI 10.1126/science.adh2586 [unverified — from recall; not confirmed by search].** Preregistered experiment, 453 college-educated professionals; ChatGPT cut task time 40% and raised quality 18%. *Why it matters:* the canonical productivity-effect citation. — [Marginal Revolution summary](https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2023/07/shakked-noy-and-whitney-zhang-on-the-productivity-effects-of-llms.html)
- **Doshi & Hauser (2024). "Generative AI enhances individual creativity but reduces the collective diversity of novel content." Science Advances 10(28):eadn5290. DOI 10.1126/sciadv.adn5290 [verified]. OA (PMC11244532; data on Dryad).** Writers given LLM story ideas produced stories rated more creative/better written/more enjoyable, largest gains for less-creative writers, but stories were more similar to one another. *Why it matters:* "individual gain vs collective homogenisation" is the framing that lets a drummer argue embodied, idiosyncratic performance preserves diversity. — [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11244532); [Exeter ORE](https://ore.exeter.ac.uk/articles/journal_contribution/Generative_AI_enhances_individual_creativity_but_reduces_the_collective_diversity_of_novel_content/29809991); [Dryad](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi%3A10.5061/dryad.qfttdz0pm)
- **Zhou & Lee (2024). "Generative artificial intelligence, human creativity, and art." PNAS Nexus 3(3):pgae052. DOI 10.1093/pnasnexus/pgae052 [verified]. OA (PMC10914360).** >4 M artworks, >50 k users: text-to-image AI raised creative productivity ~25% and favourites-per-view ~50%; peak content novelty up, average down; artists who filter outputs benefit most; AI adoption reduced concentration of value capture. *Why it matters:* the only large-scale *market-value* measurement of AI-augmented art; template for measuring "value per view" of XR performances. — [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10914360); [BU summary](https://www.bu.edu/questrom/blog/eric-zhou-dokyun-lee-generative-artificial-intelligence-human-creativity-and-art)
- **Brynjolfsson, Li & Raymond (2025). "Generative AI at Work." Quarterly Journal of Economics 140(2):889–942. DOI 10.1093/qje/qjae044 [verified]. arXiv:2304.11771.** 5,172 customer-support agents; AI assistance raised productivity 15% on average, most for less-experienced workers. *Why it matters:* top-journal anchor for "AI augments the practitioner" claims in a grant. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.11771); [EconPapers](https://econpapers.repec.org/paper/eclstabus/4141.htm)
- **Horton, White & Iyengar (2023). "Bias against AI art can enhance perceptions of human creativity." Scientific Reports 13. DOI 10.1038/s41598-023-45202-3 [verified]. OA (PMC10624838).** Six experiments (N = 2,965): art labelled AI-made valued less even when indistinguishable or human-collaborated; side-by-side exposure raised perceived human creativity. *Why it matters:* predicts that *visible human embodiment* (a drummer on stage) is where value accrues — core hypothesis for "embodied AI creativity". — [PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10624838/); [Columbia Business School](https://business.columbia.edu/faculty/research/bias-against-ai-art-can-enhance-perceptions-human-creativity)
- **Shank, Stefanik, Stuhlsatz, Kacirek & Belfi (2023). "AI Composer Bias: Listeners Like Music Less When They Think It Was Composed by an AI." Journal of Experimental Psychology: Applied 29(3). DOI 10.1037/xap0000447 [verified]. Online 2022-08-25.** Three studies: listeners like music less when told it is AI-composed (classical excerpts), no effect for electronic music. *Why it matters:* music-specific attribution bias; suggests acoustic/physical drumming may be insulated from the penalty. — [Missouri S&T Scholars' Mine](https://scholarsmine.mst.edu/psysci_facwork/254); [PDF mirror](https://www.Gwern.net/doc/ai/music/2022-shank.pdf)
- A 2025 arXiv review notes evidence on AI-composer bias is mixed; Millet et al. (2023) found listeners felt less awe and attributed less creativity to music believed AI-composed (full Millet citation unverified; likely Computers in Human Behavior 2023). — [arXiv 2512.02785](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.02785)
- **"Valuing AI Art: An Experimental Study" — AEA RCT Registry, DOI 10.1257/rct.14469-1.0.** Registered (not yet reported) lab-auction design measuring willingness-to-pay for human vs AI art. *Why it matters:* shows WTP auction designs are the emerging standard for AI-art valuation — adaptable to performance tickets. — [AEA registry](https://www.socialscienceregistry.org/trials/14469)
- **"Humans versus AI: whether and why we prefer human-created compared to AI-created artwork." Cognitive Research: Principles and Implications (c. 2023) [full citation/DOI unverified].** AI paintings labelled human got higher ratings; gap attributed to perceived narrative and effort. — [Cambridge repository](https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/handle/1810/354873)

### Inferences
- Combining Zhou & Lee's "value per view" with Horton/Shank's attribution-bias designs yields a feasible study: randomise disclosure of AI involvement across XR drum performance screenings and measure WTP/attendance intention — this would be the first such study for live music (no contrary evidence found).

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed study measuring box-office, merchandise, streaming or sponsorship impact of AI-augmented live performance was found.
- Dell'Acqua et al. (2023, "jagged frontier") and Krueger's *Rockonomics* (2019) / Connolly & Krueger (2006) are well-known but were not searched/verified in this session — treat as unverified recall.
- Auslander's *Liveness* (Routledge; 3rd ed. 2023) is the standard theory text on the value of live vs mediated performance; not verified here.

## Key Question 5 — Studies specifically on drummers/percussionists with AI partners (Shimon/Haile, Georgia Tech, Weinberg; others)

### Takeaway
Georgia Tech's Robotic Musicianship group (Weinberg) is the canonical lineage — Haile the robotic percussionist (2005–06), Shimon the marimba improviser (2009–), the wearable "third arm" drumming limb (2016), Shimon the rapper (2020) — consolidated in the 2020 Springer monograph whose subtitle literally contains "Embodied Artificial Creativity", making it the obvious anchor for the proposed title.

## KQ1. Online course platforms used by Japanese creators — revenue share, typical prices, disclosed creator earnings

### Takeaway
Official take-rates are now reasonably well documented: ストアカ 10% (self-sourced) / 20% (in-person, ストアカ-sourced) / 30% (online, ストアカ-sourced), MENTA 20% ex-tax (15% platform + 5% payment) + ¥300 per payout, Udemy 37% on marketplace sales vs 97% on instructor-coupon sales (subscription pool stepping down 20%→17.5%→15% in Jan 2024/2025/2026), ココナラ 22% (27.5% for video-chat from 2025-04-16). Coloso and Schoo do **not** publish instructor revenue-share; Coloso course prices are ~¥20,000–¥42,500 one-time per third-party measurements. No official Japanese per-instructor earnings disclosures were found for any platform.

### Cited Findings

**Udemy (global + Japan)**
- Official revenue share: instructors receive **97%** of net revenue when the student buys via the instructor's own coupon/referral link; **37%** when no instructor link was used (Udemy ads, marketplace browsing). "Net Amount" = price paid less taxes and fees such as the 30% Apple/Google app-store fee. — [Udemy Support: Instructor revenue share](https://support.udemy.com/hc/en-us/articles/229605008) [snippet, page not opened]
- Udemy's subscription (Udemy Business / Personal Plan) revenue pool for instructors steps down: **20% in Jan 2024, 17.5% in Jan 2025, 15% in Jan 2026**; marketplace rate of 37% unchanged (per CEO letter quoted in the Japanese instructor community). — [Udemy Community JP, Feb 2024 Instructor Insider](https://community.udemy.com/ja/discussion/146995/2024%E5%B9%B42%E6%9C%88%E3%81%AEudemy-instructor-insider) [snippet]
- Class Central (third-party) estimates effective instructor share fell from 40% (2019) to ~24% (2024) — an outside estimate, not official. — [Class Central report](https://classcentral.com/report/?p=94577) [snippet]
- Udemy's Japan-specific terms update post (date not shown in snippet): typical Japan instructor revenue mix cited as "Udemy Organic ~60%, Marketing boost (affiliate/ads) ~30%, Instructor promotion ~10%"; Udemy claims the simplification lets some instructors "take home as much as 27% more"; worked example under *old* terms: ¥100,000 monthly net revenue → ~¥36,900 instructor take-home. — [Teach Udemy: Updates to T&C for Japan-Based Instructors](https://teach.udemy.com/japan-tos-updates/) [snippet; the search summary's claim of a "50% organic share" could not be confirmed and should be treated as unverified]
- Forbes Japan (2021) reported the instructor share on Udemy's non-referral sales was cut from a former maximum of 50% to 37%. — [Forbes Japan article 44120](https://forbesjapan.com/articles/detail/44120) [snippet]
- Benesse invested ~¥5.5bn in Udemy Inc. in Feb 2020 (Benesse is Udemy's Japan partner). — [M&A Capital Partners news](https://www.ma-cp.com/news/8559/) [snippet]
- Japanese price reality (blog, not official): sale prices are typically ¥1,200–¥2,400 and list-price sales are rare; blog estimate: 30 sales/month at ¥1,800 sale price ≈ ¥20,000 instructor revenue/month. — [atsoho blog: udemy-instructor-side](https://atsoho.com/blog/udemy-instructor-side); [atsoho blog: udemy-course-creation-fukugyo](https://atsoho.com/blog/udemy-course-creation-fukugyo) [blog, snippet]
- Anecdotal JP earnings: a bootcamp promo claims several instructors earned ¥100,000+ (some ¥200,000+) within months of launch (promotional claim); one blogger reports ¥180,000+ in month two after a June 2021 launch. — [Lancers Lab](https://www.lancers.jp/lab/detail/386); [atsoho: how-to-become-udemy-online-instructor-guide](https://atsoho.com/blog/how-to-become-udemy-online-instructor-guide) [promotional/blog, snippet]

- Older Lifehacker JP article (2018) said 10–20% fee; now superseded by the official 10/20/30 structure above. — [Lifehacker JP 2018](https://www.lifehacker.jp/2018/05/how-to-become-teacher-in-street-academy.html) [snippet]

**MENTA**

Sources conflict on whether a free tier still exists. — [schoolmaker.com](https://schoolmaker.com/blog/thinkific-pricing); [learniverse.app](https://www.learniverse.app/blog/thinkific-vs-teachable-pricing) [snippet]
- Japanese TouchDesigner creator example of self-hosting: "Okamirufu" sells a 2025 beginner TouchDesigner course on Teachable for **$137**. — [okamirufu-s-school.teachable.com](https://okamirufu-s-school.teachable.com) [snippet]

### Inferences
- For a niche skill set (XR drumming, TouchDesigner, Vision Pro), the fee-efficient path is: ストアカ/MENTA with **self-sourced traffic (10% / 20%)** or Teachable Builder (0% fee), rather than Udemy marketplace (37% on net, further reduced in subscription pool).
- Coloso's price point (¥20k–¥42.5k one-time) implies much higher per-sale revenue than Udemy's JP sale prices (¥1,200–¥2,400), but Coloso is curated/invite-based and its share is undisclosed.
- MENTA's "¥14,000 average contract" is a useful anchor for pricing 1:1 coaching plans.

### Gaps
- No Coloso instructor revenue-share %, contract terms, or instructor payout cases found (Coloso FAQ not reachable).
- No Schoo honorarium figures found.
- No official per-instructor earnings data from Udemy Japan; only promotional/blog anecdotes.
- The teach.udemy.com Japan post's publication date and exact new split were not visible in snippets.

## KQ3. University / vocational-school guest lectures and part-time teaching — pay benchmarks with sources

### Takeaway
Part-time university teaching (非常勤講師) is paid per コマ: typical public figures are ¥11,000–¥16,100 per 90-min コマ-month (art/design universities), or hourly ¥3,650–¥6,450 (national-university tables); a 1-コマ/week year contract is ~¥150,000–¥400,000/year. One-off guest lectures (講演謝金) at national universities are capped by 謝金規程 at roughly **¥12,000–¥20,000/hour** (ゲストスピーカー ¥7,000/h at Osaka U; ¥50,000 per 2-hour 一般講演 at Osaka Kyoiku U); government standard tables put 大学教授級 at ¥7,000–¥8,700/h.
